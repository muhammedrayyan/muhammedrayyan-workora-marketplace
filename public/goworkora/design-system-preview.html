System states

No blank or ambiguous screens.

Every data-driven page reserves deliberate loading, empty, success and recoverable error states.

···

Loading

Preserve the page structure while content is resolved.

○

Empty

Explain what is absent and provide one useful next action.

✓

Success

Confirm the completed action and its resulting state.

!

Recoverable error

Use plain language, preserve input and offer a safe retry.
